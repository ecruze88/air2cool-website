## What to Do When Your AC Stops Working in Randolph

Start at the thermostat. Confirm that it is set to cool and the desired temperature is below the room temperature. If the screen is blank, replace batteries if your thermostat uses them and check the HVAC breaker.

Check the filter. Randolph has a mix of newer and older homes, and both can suffer from airflow problems when filters clog. A dirty filter can cause weak airflow, frozen coils, longer run times, and warm air from the vents.

Inspect the outdoor unit from a safe distance. Make sure it is not blocked by grass, shrubs, leaves, or patio items. Do not remove panels, force the fan blade, or repeatedly reset a breaker that trips.

## Most Common Reasons AC Stops Working in NJ Summer

Capacitors fail often during hot weather. The outdoor unit may hum, click, or refuse to start. Since capacitors help motors start and run, a weak one can stop cooling abruptly.

Low refrigerant or a refrigerant leak can make the system run constantly while barely cooling. Ice on the refrigerant line, weak airflow, and high humidity indoors are common signs. We check the system before recommending next steps.

Frozen evaporator coils happen when airflow is restricted or refrigerant is low. The AC may seem like it is running, but the house keeps getting warmer. We thaw and test the system safely before restarting it.

Fan motor, contactor, and wiring failures can keep the outdoor unit from running. Air2Cool carries common parts and works to complete same-day emergency repairs whenever possible.
