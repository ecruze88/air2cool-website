## What to Do When Your AC Stops Working in Morristown

First, check the thermostat. Make sure it is set to cool and the desired temperature is lower than the room temperature. If the display is blank, replace batteries if applicable and confirm the HVAC breaker is on.

Second, check the breaker once. If the AC breaker has tripped, reset it one time. If it trips again, do not keep forcing it. Repeated trips can mean a failing capacitor, motor, compressor, or wiring problem that needs professional diagnosis.

Third, look at the filter and outdoor unit. Dirty filters are common in older Morristown homes and condos with compact mechanical closets. Outside, make sure the condenser is clear of debris and not blocked by landscaping. Do not open electrical panels, chip ice off the coil, or keep restarting a noisy unit.

## Most Common Reasons AC Stops Working in NJ Summer

Failed capacitors are a frequent emergency repair. You may hear humming, clicking, or notice that the outdoor fan will not spin. This part works hard during heat waves and often fails under peak load.

Low refrigerant or a refrigerant leak can make the AC run constantly without cooling. Ice on the line, weak cooling, and high humidity indoors are warning signs. Our technicians check pressures and look for the reason the charge is low.

Frozen coils are common when airflow is restricted. Historic homes and renovated spaces around Morristown sometimes have older ductwork or tight returns, which can make airflow problems worse. We thaw the system safely and identify the root cause.
