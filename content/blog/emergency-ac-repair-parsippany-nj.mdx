## What to Do When Your AC Stops Working in Parsippany

Check the thermostat first. Make sure the system is set to cool and the temperature setting is below the current indoor temperature. If the display is blank, replace batteries if applicable and check the breaker.

Inspect the air filter. Parsippany homes near busy roads and wooded areas can collect dust and pollen quickly. A clogged filter can reduce airflow enough to freeze the coil and leave the system blowing warm air.

Look outside at the condenser. Clear leaves, grass, and debris from around the unit, but do not remove electrical panels or reach into the fan area. If the outdoor unit is buzzing, silent, or repeatedly tripping the breaker, shut it off and call a technician.

Call immediately if the house is heating up during high humidity, the system is iced over, the breaker trips more than once, or the AC is running but not cooling. We provide upfront pricing before any work begins.

## Most Common Reasons AC Stops Working in NJ Summer

Capacitor failure is a frequent cause of sudden AC breakdowns. The unit may click, hum, or fail to start. This repair is often completed the same day when the rest of the system checks out.

Low refrigerant and refrigerant leaks can cause warm air, poor humidity control, and ice on the refrigerant line. A proper diagnosis matters because simply adding refrigerant without addressing the leak can leave you with the same problem again.

Frozen evaporator coils happen when airflow is restricted or refrigerant is low. The AC may run for hours without cooling. Air2Cool checks filters, blower operation, coil condition, and refrigerant readings to find the cause.

An outdoor unit that will not run may have a bad contactor, failed fan motor, damaged wiring, or control issue. Our technicians carry common parts and work to restore cooling the same day whenever possible.
