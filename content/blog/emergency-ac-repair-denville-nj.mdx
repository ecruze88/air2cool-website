## What to Do When Your AC Stops Working in Denville

Start with the thermostat. Confirm it is set to cool and that the target temperature is below the current room temperature. If the thermostat is blank, replace batteries if it uses them and check the breaker panel.

Check the air filter next. Denville homes near wooded or lake areas can see heavy pollen, dust, and moisture, which means filters may clog faster than expected. A clogged filter can restrict airflow until the coil freezes and the system stops cooling.

Look at the outdoor condenser. Make sure leaves, grass, and brush are not blocking airflow around the unit. Do not remove electrical covers, reach into the fan area, or keep resetting a breaker that trips more than once.
